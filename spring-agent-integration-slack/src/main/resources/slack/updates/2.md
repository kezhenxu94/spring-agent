---
title: You can pick the model that answers you
---
Type `/config` and a modal opens, private to you: choose among the models this deployment already pays for, or add an OpenAI-compatible endpoint of your own and have your chats answered by that instead. Everybody else stays on the default. Nothing on that path goes through a model, so it still works when the one you picked stops answering, and your token is stored sealed and never shown again.

If nothing happens, the command was not declared on the Slack app — send ` /config` with a leading space and the same form opens here in the channel.
