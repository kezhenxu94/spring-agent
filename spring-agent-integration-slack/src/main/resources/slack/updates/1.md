---
title: Answering in threads
---
I stream my answer into a single message as I write it, and keep that message up to date with what I am doing: the tools I am calling and what they returned, my task list while I work through one, and what the turn cost.

Two things worth knowing. *Stop* ends a run — only for the person it is answering, so pressing it in a channel cannot cut somebody else off. And a message you send while I am still working is not ignored: it gets a :eyes: to say I have seen it and a :white_check_mark: when I have actually read it into what I am doing, so a correction reaches the run you are watching rather than starting a second one.
