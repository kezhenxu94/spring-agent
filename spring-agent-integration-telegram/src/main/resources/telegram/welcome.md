I am an agent you can talk to here. Message me directly, or mention me in a group and I will answer you there.

While I work you will see what I am doing — what I am reading, what I am running, and what it cost. If I go the wrong way, press *Stop*, or just send another message: I will read it and take it into account rather than starting again.

If I need something from you, I will ask, and the conversation carries on when you answer — even if that is tomorrow. Send /config to choose which model answers you.
