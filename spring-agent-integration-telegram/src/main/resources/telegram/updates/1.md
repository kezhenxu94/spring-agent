---
title: Answering on Telegram
---
I answer in a single message that I keep up to date as I write: what I am thinking, the tools I am calling, my task list while I work through one, and what the turn cost. A long answer carries on in the messages under it.

*Stop* ends a run — only for the person it is answering. A message you send while I am still working gets a 👀 to say I have seen it and a 👌 when I have read it into what I am doing. Put `/kb` in a message to have me answer only from what you and your team have told me to remember, or `/mini` for a quick aside that leaves no trace.
