---
title: You can say what kind of answer you want
---
Put `/kb` in a message and I answer that one question out of what you and your team have told me to
remember, and out of nothing else — no web, no shell, no files, no connected servers — so you can go
and check where every part of it came from. Put `/mini` in one and I answer out of my own head
alone, for a quick aside you would rather not have in the thread; that one leaves no trace, so my
next answer will not know you asked. The word can sit anywhere in the message, capitals make no
difference, and `/knowledge-base` and `/one-off` are the longer spellings of the two.
