---
title: You can pick the model that answers you
---
Send `/config` and a card opens: choose among the models this deployment already pays for, or add
an OpenAI-compatible endpoint of your own and have your chats answered by that instead. Everybody
else stays on the default. Nothing on that path goes through a model, so it still works when the
one you picked stops answering. Your token is stored sealed and never shown again.
